## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an ultra-refined, warm luxury editorial aesthetic tailored for high-tier celebrations, bespoke weddings, and momentous gatherings. It marries the atmospheric visual storytelling of Airbnb with the surgical typographic precision of Linear and the effortless transaction clarity of Stripe. 

The emotional tone balances opulence with calm mastery:
- **Atmospheric & Tactile:** Rich ivory and champagne tones replace sterile enterprise starkness, imparting the sensory weight of heavy cotton paper, letterpress invitations, and bespoke event styling.
- **Architectural Precision:** Structural layouts are disciplined, razor-sharp, and unhurried. Generous negative space acts as a framing device for rich imagery and complex planning data.
- **Understated Opulence:** High-contrast accents of Deep Burgundy and artisanal Terracotta provide deliberate focal points without falling into ostentation. Every surface feels curatorial, intentional, and composed.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from heritage natural pigments, warm textile fibers, and classical event decor:

- **Primary Accent (`#B84B32` - Terracotta):** Used for focal conversion points, interactive emphasis, primary CTAs, and active navigation indicators. Conveys warmth, energy, and artisanal craftsmanship.
- **Secondary Accent (`#542D38` - Deep Burgundy):** Royal, grounding, and prestigious. Applied to executive headers, VIP tags, high-value status pills, and celebratory tier designations.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#899681` - Sage Green):** Botanical and calming. Serves as functional validation, vendor confirmations, budget milestones achieved, and operational readiness cues.
- **Muted Rose (`#D8A39B`):** Soft decorative tint for highlight badges, soft states, event timelines, and secondary interactive feedback.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - *Canvas Background (`#F8F5EF` - Ivory):* Warm, soft base substrate mimicking tactile archival paper.
  - *Card & Elevated Surface (`#FFFEFC` - Warm White):* Crisp, luminous card planes providing optical lift.
  - *Subtle Fill & Alt Sections (`#EDE5D8` - Soft Champagne):* Inset panels, disabled control tracks, and table header bands.
- **Typography & Boundaries:**
  - *Primary Neutral (`#282522` - Deep Charcoal):* High-legibility text tone carrying rich warmth, avoiding cold digital blacks.
  - *Secondary Neutral (`#817B73` - Warm Grey):* Subtitles, meta-tags, helper annotations, and inactive labels.
  - *Structural Border (`#E7E0D7`):* Low-contrast hairline dividers that maintain clean compartmentalization without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typography creates a high-contrast editorial rhythm. Headlines evoke literary poise and classical stationery, while the functional sans-serif ensures crystalline data legibility in dense matrices, vendor contracts, and scheduling timelines.

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy:** Rendered in an expressive, literary serif typeface (`Newsreader`). Optical sizing and fluid line-heights deliver grace without overwhelming adjacent UI panels. It brings editorial warmth to event titles, venue names, and milestone celebrations.
- **Interface & Data Engine:** Powered by `Manrope`. Selected for its geometric balance, humanized stroke terminals, and numerical clarity.
- **Financial & Numerical Presentation:** Monetary values utilizing the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) use tabular lining figures with snug letter tracking to preserve balance inside budget meters, vendor split rows, and cost allocations.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes an editorial 12-column grid system calibrated to support dense enterprise workflows alongside spacious visual moodboards.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns with generous 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a 48px (`3rem`) page margin. Max content boundary is capped at 1440px to ensure effortless eye tracking.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Two-column cards consolidate into unified vertical blocks.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) outer canvas margins. Multi-column metric displays transform into horizontally scrollable carousels or stacked inspection sheets.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** An 8-point base scale governs all vertical cadence and macro padding, while 4px micro-increments resolve tight status pills, button interiors, and financial table cells.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established through nuanced, atmospheric layering rather than aggressive drop shadows. The design emphasizes natural light, warmth, and physical substrate depth:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Foundation):* Canvas Ivory (`#F8F5EF`), grounding the entire application viewport.
  - *Tier 1 (Resting Cards):* Warm White (`#FFFEFC`) bordered by a 1px hairline stroke (`#E7E0D7`). Flat against the background with no drop shadow, preserving quiet, paper-like stillness.
  - *Tier 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):* Lifted by an ultra-diffused, ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(40, 37, 34, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(40, 37, 34, 0.02)`.
  - *Tier 3 (Modals, Overlays, AI Generation Panels):* Elevated with deep editorial poise: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(40, 37, 34, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(40, 37, 34, 0.03)`. Backed by an Ivory translucent backdrop filter (`rgba(248, 245, 239, 0.8)` with an 8px blur).
- **Hairline Framing:** Micro-borders are rendered strictly at 1px using `#E7E0D7`. When bordering interactive inputs in focus, the border shifts to `#B84B32` with a 3px soft outer ring of `rgba(184, 75, 50, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs an intentional duality in its shape geometry: pure architectural rectangles for macro structures contrasted with tailored, ergonomic pill geometry for interactive moments.

- **Outer Enclosures & Structural Cards:** Large containers, layout columns, and visual media cards use structured radii (`0.75rem` to `1rem`), providing modern framing for wedding venue renders, seating maps, and guest lists.
- **Interactive Pill System:** Action items, tags, filter chips, and primary buttons use pure pill curves (`9999px`), imparting a soft, luxurious feel reminiscent of smooth river stones and bespoke wax seals.
- **Micro UI Elements:** Checkboxes, subtle dropdown selectors, and notification badges leverage softer transitions (`0.375rem`) to remain grounded and precise.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), clad in Terracotta (`#B84B32`) with text in Warm White (`#FFFEFC`). Features 0.75rem vertical and 1.5rem horizontal padding, medium font-weight (`label-md`). Hover state gently deepens to `#A33E27` with subtle scale transition.
- **Secondary / Royal Accent:** Pill shape utilizing Deep Burgundy (`#542D38`) with Warm White typography, reserved for high-level operations (e.g., "Confirm Itinerary", "Export Call Sheet").
- **Ghost / Editorial Outline:** 1px hairline border (`#E7E0D7`) over Warm White surface, text in Deep Charcoal (`#282522`). On hover, the surface shifts to Soft Champagne (`#EDE5D8`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Date Pickers:** Enclosed in Warm White (`#FFFEFC`) with 1px border (`#E7E0D7`). 0.75rem padding with slightly rounded corners (`0.5rem`). Placeholder text set in Warm Grey (`#817B73`). On focus, transition border to Terracotta (`#B84B32`) with a subtle terracotta wash ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hairline bordered rounded squares (4px radius) or circular nodes. Active state fills with Deep Burgundy (`#542D38`) housing an Ivory check glyph.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Tags:** Compact pill components (`space-xs` vertical, `space-md` horizontal). Inactive state rests on `#EDE5D8` with `#817B73` text. Active state adopts Deep Charcoal (`#282522`) with Warm White text, or Terracotta for primary dimensions.
- **Status Indicators:** Pill badges displaying vendor verification, guest RSVP, or budget checks. Success instances utilize Sage Green tint (`rgba(137, 150, 129, 0.12)`) with `#4E5C47` text.

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Editorial Cards:** Crafted from Warm White (`#FFFEFC`), framed with 1px `#E7E0D7` micro-borders, devoid of harsh shadows. Cards prioritize deep content margins (`space-lg`), pairing serif titles with crisp sans-serif metadata.
- **Financial Matrices (INR Breakdown):** Budget cards featuring tabular numbers, deep charcoal currency labels (`₹`), and sage progress fills.

### AI Co-Pilot & Generation Interfaces
- **Gemini Intelligence Drawer:** Surfaces feature an ultra-fine linear border tinted with a subtle gradient of Muted Rose (`#D8A39B`) and Deep Burgundy (`#542D38`). AI prompt response cards rest on a tinted Champagne ground (`#EDE5D8` at 50% opacity) with Newsreader italic typography for generated suggestions.
